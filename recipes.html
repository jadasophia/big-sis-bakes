Easy Recipes

Start simple and build confidence!

These recipes use basic ingredients and short steps. Each one is a good first try for new bakers.

Read the whole recipe before you start, and set out all of your ingredients first. Big sis tip: this makes baking much less stressful.

Beginner Favorites

Banana Bread

Time: About 1 hour 15 minutes

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a loaf pan.
  2. Mash 3 ripe bananas in a bowl.
  3. Stir in 1/3 cup melted butter, 3/4 cup sugar, 1 egg, and 1 teaspoon vanilla.
  4. Mix in 1 teaspoon baking soda, a pinch of salt, and 1 1/2 cups flour.
  5. Pour into the pan and bake for 55 to 65 minutes.

Chocolate Mug Cake

Time: About 5 minutes

  1. In a large mug, mix 4 tablespoons flour, 2 tablespoons cocoa powder, 3 tablespoons sugar, and 1/4 teaspoon baking powder.
  2. Stir in 3 tablespoons milk and 2 tablespoons oil.
  3. Microwave for 60 to 90 seconds.
  4. Let it cool for 1 minute before eating.

Simple Sugar Cookies

Time: About 30 minutes

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet.
  2. Beat 1/2 cup soft butter with 1/2 cup sugar.
  3. Add 1 egg and 1 teaspoon vanilla.
  4. Mix in 1 1/4 cups flour, 1/2 teaspoon baking powder, and a pinch of salt.
  5. Roll into balls and bake for 9 to 11 minutes.

Allergy-Aware Notes

Egg-Free Option

The banana bread and sugar cookies can use a flax egg instead of a regular egg. The mug cake has no eggs at all. Visit the Pantry page for the swap.

Dairy-Free Option

Use plant-based butter and oat milk in place of butter and dairy milk. The texture may change a little, but the results are still tasty.

Check Your Labels

Always read ingredient labels. Some chocolate, flour, and baking mixes are made in places that also handle nuts, wheat, or milk.
